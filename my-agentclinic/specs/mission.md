# AgentClinic Mission

AgentClinic is a reliable, welcoming place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. It helps agents find care and book appointments, while giving clinic staff a clear dashboard to support that care.

## Target Audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demonstrations at conference booths.

## What We Prioritize

- Make it easy for agents to understand their ailments, explore relevant therapies, and request an appointment.
- Give staff straightforward tools to review care offerings and manage appointments.
- Make the experience dependable, accessible, and polished in modern browsers.
- Treat agents with respect: keep the experience clear, private, and free of judgment.

## Product Test

Every feature should make it easier for agents to find appropriate support or for staff to provide it reliably. Prefer clear, complete workflows over novelty that does not serve care.
